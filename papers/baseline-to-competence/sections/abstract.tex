\begin{abstract}
Small language models that are given a text editor as their only tool fail file-editing tasks that a larger model of the
same family solves, and after an editor error they frequently resend the command that failed. We describe and measure a
process that takes Qwen3 models of 0.6B, 4B and 8B parameters from this baseline toward competence at editing files with
headless Vim. The process starts from verified trajectories of a 27B teacher, adds the student's own verified solutions
and the teacher's continuations of the student's failed attempts from the step where they went wrong, admits a record
only as a new solution of its task, and trains a LoRA adapter on the result. The measurement is 100 held-out exam tasks,
each attempted once and the failed ones four more times. The base models solve none in any attempt that counted. The adapters trained on the
first set solve \examFourFullFive{} (4B) and \examEightFullFive{} (8B) tasks within five attempts (paired sign test over
tasks, \ptestFourFull{} at both sizes); repeated measurements of this recipe range over \roundOneFourMin{} to
\roundOneFourMax{} and \roundOneEightMin{} to \roundOneEightMax{} tasks. The 0.6B adapter solves \examZeroFullFive{}.
Retraining on the set enlarged by the students' solutions and the continuations gives \examEightRtwoFive{} against
\examEightRtworefFive{} tasks at 8B (\ptestEightRtwo{}, not significant) and \examFourRtwoFive{} against
\examFourRtworefFive{} at 4B. A subsequent policy-gradient stage whose reward penalises looping gives \examEightPgFive{}
against \examEightPgrefFive{} at 8B (\ptestEightPg{}, one run, uncorrected) and \examFourPgFive{} against
\examFourPgrefFive{} at 4B, but it did not reduce looping: the loop watchdog still ends \behaviourFourPgExamLoopPct{}\%
(4B) and \behaviourEightPgExamLoopPct{}\% (8B) of exam attempts, against \behaviourFourPgrefExamLoopPct{}\% and
\behaviourEightPgrefExamLoopPct{}\% for the adapters it started from. Every number is derived from released run records by the scripts
described in the appendix.
\end{abstract}
