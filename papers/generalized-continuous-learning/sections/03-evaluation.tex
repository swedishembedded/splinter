\section{Evaluation}\label{sec:evaluation}

The examiner draws each exam from its own program generator with a seed only it holds, excludes any item that matches a
program a candidate trained on, serves every candidate on a pinned build, and grades each answer by the interpreter's output.
The learner never controls grading: the generator, its seed, the items and their per-item results stay with the examiner.

Every generation is reported, not only the last: the learning curve over generations with intervals that account for both the
exam's program templates and the variation between independently trained lineages, the items a generation loses that its
predecessor answered (retention), the worst per-template regression, and the compute spent per generation (proposing,
answering, training and gating, in GPU-hours) beside the gain it bought. Comparisons that separate the loop from the volume of
verified data are made at matched data and, separately, at matched compute. The registered criterion, its exams and its seeds
are fixed in \texttt{prereg/} before any candidate is examined.
