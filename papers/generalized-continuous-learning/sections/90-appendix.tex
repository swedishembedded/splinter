\section{Datasets, models and builds}\label{app:artifacts}

\Cref{tab:artifacts} lists every dataset, model and evidence repository the paper relies on, at the revision its numbers come
from (full hashes in \texttt{data/artifacts.csv}); \cref{tab:builds} lists the source commits and the frozen binaries of the
Vim runs (full hashes in \texttt{data/builds.csv}). Brain's history was rewritten after the Vim runs: the binary built from
\texttt{b726c045bb34} is the tree-equivalent of upstream \texttt{d6bd3baaff53} (in parentheses); the full old-to-new map is
\nolinkurl{builds/brain-rewrite-map-2026-11-27.txt} of the Vim evidence repository. The other commits are on their
repositories' main branches. A binary named \texttt{<repo>-<commit>/<file>} is the frozen copy every run of that stage used;
its sha256 is given to 16 hex digits.

\paragraph{Where the evidence is.} Lua: the examiner's signed group verdict is \nolinkurl{data/rsi5-groups-G.json}
(thinking on: \nolinkurl{data/rsi5-groups-G-thinking.json}); adapters, lineage records and per-candidate verdicts are in
\nolinkurl{swedishembedded/generalized-continuous-learning-evidence}. Vim: aggregate evidence, the analysis code and the data
and previews of every figure are in \nolinkurl{swedishembedded/source-to-transfer-evidence}. Per-exam outcomes are item-level
exam data and stay with the examiner's private evidence. Besides the binaries of \cref{tab:builds}, splinter's run records name
the sven and brain library revisions linked into splinter (\texttt{704496d6}, \texttt{b2f46a22}). The decision is recomputed
by \texttt{decide.py} and \texttt{secondary.py} from the per-task outcomes \texttt{export-results.py} writes; the tables and
figures are made by \nolinkurl{scripts/paper_data.py} and the pictures in \nolinkurl{figures/} from \nolinkurl{data/}, whose
\nolinkurl{vim-transfer/} files are byte copies of the evidence dataset's (\texttt{make vim-data}).
