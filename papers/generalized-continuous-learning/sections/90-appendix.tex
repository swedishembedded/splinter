\section{Registrations and internal names}\label{app:registrations}

The Lua run is registered as RSI5 in \texttt{prereg/rsi5.md} (2026-11-16) with its launch record \texttt{prereg/rsi5-launch.md};
its sealed exam is the examiner's exam G and its secondary exam the companion paper's exam F; its criteria G1 (every generation
better than its predecessor; not met), G2 (generation~4 at least five points above the base; met) and G3 (gain on never-examined
families; met) are evaluated by \texttt{scripts/rsi5\_criteria.py}. \texttt{prereg/rsi6.md} and \texttt{prereg/rsi7.md} are
later registrations of this paper with no examined result yet. The Vim experiment is registered in splinter's
\texttt{.agents/roadmap/source-to-transfer.md} (commit \texttt{c319ff4c3}, 2026-11-26, before any arm trained; outcome-first arm
added in \texttt{66277f180}). Its arms are lettered there: A the base, B all verified, C one per task, D productive actions, F
the shuffled-request control, G outcome first, S student only; the held-out authored tasks are its measurement T1 and the exams
T2/T4. The evidence files keep these letters (\texttt{arm-<letter>-s<seed>}).

\section{Measurements that showed no gain, and exploratory readings}\label{app:other}

\paragraph{Lua run.} Generations 3 and 4 did not improve on their predecessors (\cref{tab:lua}); the learner's own gate
advanced every generation, including on development leads that were not significant, so it did not detect the plateau. On the
items the base already answered, every comparison involving generation~1 loses ($-37$ points on that small stratum). On exam F
(2340 items, thinking off) the lineages' mean share right was 8.4\% for the base and 15.3\%, 17.6\%, 19.2\% and 19.7\% after
generations 1--4. With thinking on (one greedy completion, 8192-token budget, descriptive), the base and the generation-4
candidates failed to finish 437 and 466--653 of the 2040 items; with 35\% failures tolerated in advance the examiner keeps the
768 items every model finished, on which the base answered 66.3\% and generation~4 63.7\% ($-2.6$ points, $[-8.1, +2.8]$,
inconclusive). Two learner-side signals were read without exam data (descriptive, after~\cite{cai2026nosecondrun}): the frontier
yield fell from 0.73--0.91 in generation~1 to 0.39--0.54 in generation~2, and the proposer's novelty collapsed in generation~4
in every lineage (0.42, 0.27, 0.56, 0.34), the generation whose step is flat.

\paragraph{Learner-side pilots on supervision (Lua, development tasks only).} Before registration, training on 4000
interpreter-labelled answers raised greedy direct-answer accuracy on 400 development programs from 0.535 to 0.695 (0.750 after a
second round), while training on the base's own interpreter-confirmed reasoning traces, in the manner
of~\cite{zelikman2022star,singh2023rest}, lowered thinking-enabled accuracy from 0.402 to 0.31--0.34 at each of three learning
rates. After the run, supervising reasoning with the interpreter's execution trace, raw or as sentences, lowered thinking-enabled
accuracy against answer-only training (0.69 and 0.72 against 0.78) while cutting tokens per correct answer about eightfold.
These fixed the choice of answer-only supervision; they are not examined results.

\section{Defects found and fixed during the Vim run}\label{app:failures}

\begin{itemize}
\item Admission read Vim's output in text mode and ran Vim under a locale the task image lacks (bench \texttt{f73ae591},
  bench knowledge 086; \cref{sec:r-admission}).
\item Sandbox images held in memory exhausted a node (splinter \texttt{851216e17}, knowledge 225), forcing the fork of the
  material run; a resumed batch could not reconcile (\texttt{5fb824d6b}); attempts lost to interruptions spent retries
  (\texttt{cc5581333}).
\item On-disk sandbox copies took 436~s to start (shared lower tree plus overlay, \texttt{9841886df}, knowledge 227); the
  gateway's connection pool, Harbor's job lock and a per-command lookup took about 50 of 70~s per trial (\texttt{9415eb39b},
  \texttt{2b62ff444}, \texttt{de1149b0a}, \texttt{418046bd7}, knowledge 228).
\item Brain's router stranded requests behind hung upstreams (\texttt{ae72fc212}) and packed one model on few cards
  (\texttt{b726c045b}); Qwen3 serving plans withdrew a model instead of fitting free memory (\texttt{f74314255}); a CUDA lane
  can hang while \texttt{/health} reports ok (brain knowledge 602; no watchdog yet).
\item Of the teacher's 2820 attempts at the 705 outcome-first end states, 1498 ran out of wall-clock time; 143 end states were
  never reached and are not tasks.
\end{itemize}
