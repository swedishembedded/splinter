\section{Verdict and limitations}\label{sec:limitations}

\Cref{tab:verdict} holds each family and model size against the operational definition of \cref{sec:introduction}. What is
supported is continuous gain over two generations in the Lua family, and in the Vim family a first generation that beats the
base at both sizes, with its gain coming from the teacher's trajectories, followed at both sizes by a second generation that did
not improve on the first; not continuous self-improvement. After the generation-2 exam results had been seen, a human changed generation~3's training budget
(LoRA steps scaled with the dataset instead of a fixed cap; \cref{app:deviations}). That is an intervention between
generations: it counts against the no-intervention part of the definition, and a generation-3 result from a scaled-steps
candidate cannot support the criterion as registered; it is shown as its own labelled point.

\paragraph{Limitations.} Each family has one task protocol; the learners are Qwen3-4B (both families) and Qwen3-8B (Vim), with
rank-16 adapters. The Lua gains shrink with
each doubling of the pool (5.1, 1.6, 1.2, 0.3 points) and there is no matched-data control. In the Vim family the absolute level
is low (3.5\% of exam attempts for the best arm, against the teacher's 63\% first-attempt rate); generation~1's gain comes from the 27B teacher's trajectories, and learning from the
learner's own attempts is not shown; the comparison of authoring modes is confounded with passes per record and task difficulty; the 100 exams come
from one course and are public, so a model trained on data gathered after their publication could have seen them; the per-seed intervals of \cref{fig:vim-seeds} treat attempts as independent. Other tools and domains, and
stability under another authoring seed, are not measured.

\paragraph{Reproducibility.} Authoring tasks from the manual needs bench (its authoring operators, prompts and schemas, the
manual's text and the recipes); bench is not published. Everything after authoring (oracle admission, attempts, the training
sets, training, the exam and held-out evaluations and the analysis) runs from the published repositories of \cref{tab:artifacts}
and splinter, as the reproduction procedure in the appendix shows.
