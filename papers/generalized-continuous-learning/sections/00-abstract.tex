\begin{abstract}
This paper asks whether a local model can keep improving, generation after generation, on a task family whose only teacher is
an executable environment, with every generation judged by a separate examiner on exams the learner never sees. It is a draft:
its main experiment (RSI5: four generations of four lineages, judged on a fresh sealed exam with 58 never-examined program
families) is registered and running, and no examined result is reported yet.
\end{abstract}
