# Pre-registration: the revised readers on a third utterance set (T1, T3)

On the second set (`prereg/teaching-reading-b.md`) the plain reader fired once on "Next time maybe we'll try the other supplier" and the model reader fired on four non-teachings (a request phrased as a question, a one-time exception, a report of what someone said, a two-step request). Two changes were made after seeing those errors and are the only changes:
the plain reader refuses a rule whose instruction begins with a hedge word (maybe, perhaps, probably, possibly, might, could, would, should); and the model reader's prompt says that a short sequence of actions now, a one-time instruction, a question about whether to do something and a report of what someone else said or will do are not teaching.
Because they were made on the second set, that set no longer tests the readers. The third set (`data/teaching/utterances-c.jsonl`: 100 utterances, 22 facts, 22 rules, 12 procedures, 44 non-teachings of which at least 20 near-misses) was written by a separate agent whose brief described the kinds in words and gave no example phrasings; it was not shown any set or the readers.
Reported once on all 100: teachings found and misread, non-teachings fired on, per reader, with 95% Wilson intervals. The first and second sets are re-scored with the revised readers as a regression check and are not evidence.
Limit: written by a model; 44 non-teachings cannot show a false-reading rate below about 8% at 95% even with none.
