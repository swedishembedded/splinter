\section{What did not work before S4}\label{sec:earlier}

\Cref{tab:rsi-all} lists every registered test; \cref{app:series} gives each with its numbers. S1 (one
registration with dated arms, graded by its own scripts before the examiner existed) found that gains on
the loop's own probes were mostly adaptation to the probing protocol, built the exam, and showed large
first steps on it with self-proposed programs labelled by the interpreter; third steps did not hold
within a lineage, and arms that replaced the labels with rewards on the policy's own attempts (self-imitation
of reasoning, policy gradient) did not move the exam.\cl{RS11,RS12,RS15,RS17} S2, on-policy policy
gradient, moved the exam by 2 to 4 items per lineage at generation~1 and no step held.\cl{RS19} S3 trained
a continued adapter on the champion's greedy mispredictions: all four lineages improved at generations 1
and 2, but at generation 3 one held flat and one fell, and over the four lineages the mispredicted share
of proposals fell from 2127 of 2400 to 860 of 1942.\cl{RS22,RS23} S4 therefore retrained from the base
on a growing frontier. Because that design search used one exam, S4 was re-examined on a fresh one
(\cref{sec:followups}).\cl{CT22,FU01}
