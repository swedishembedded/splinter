\section{The headline experiment and its replication: registered, not yet run}\label{sec:pending}

\paragraph{The claim this paper exists to establish.} Verified engineering-agent experience
can produce a sustained improvement in completion of previously unseen repository tasks,
under a controlled compute budget and without unacceptable loss of earlier capability.\cl{PR09}
\textbf{That claim is not yet measured.} No coding-task experiment has been run; this
section states exactly what will be run, what each outcome will mean, and where its numbers
will appear. Nothing in it is a result.

The experiments are registered in \code{prereg/coding-tasks.md}, dated 2026-10-16 and written
before any of their runs, and amended once, also before any run, on 2026-10-16 at 22:00 CEST
(\cref{sec:amend}). \Cref{tab:registered} lists them. Their
numbers will be read from \code{data/results.csv} by \code{scripts/build_data.py}; a row exists
only for a finished run, and the status column below changes by itself when one does.\cl{PL02}

\begin{description}
\item[CE1, the untrained agent.] The base agent's resolve rate on repository tasks, its failure
profile, its cost per resolved task and the discordance between the base and the 27B teacher per
family, which is the sizing input for the qualification partition (greedy decoding makes two seeds of one arm identical). No threshold; nothing is trained before it is
reported.\cl{PR10}
\item[CE4, the headline (as amended).] A LoRA adapter on Qwen3-8B trained only on verified
trajectories, produced by the 27B teacher served as the base, from
development-partition families, compared with the untrained 8B on the qualification partition by the
primary test of \cref{sec:protocol}, served through Brain and driven by Sven, with retention,
serve-verdict agreement and the three controls. The headline holds only if the sign test has
$p<0.05$, retention is not refused and agreement is within the serve check's tolerance; any
other outcome is reported as what it is, including ``no held-out improvement'' and
``memorisation''.\cl{PR11,PR15}
\item[CE6, the 27B model.] Ten stages from loading the exact model to comparing correctness,
tool-call validity and resources against the base, gated by training correctness and by
deployment agreement. The 27B is not used for a confirmatory query until both gates pass;
the training rate of \cref{sec:throughput} is reported as measured and no target is
registered.\cl{PR12}
\item[CE7, replication.] The unchanged CE1 and CE4 procedure on a second, independent
repository chosen by a rule fixed in advance (permissive licence, offline build, a suite of
bounded running time, no part of the pipeline tuned on it), with the cross cells that separate
learning a repository from learning the protocol. The headline counts as reproduced only if
both repositories pass the primary test independently.\cl{PR13}
\item[CE8, 27B self-improvement.] The 27B model fine-tuned on its own verified trajectories,
compared with the 27B base on the qualification partition by the same primary test, retention
and controls. It runs only after the 27B adapter serving path exists (CE6 passes its training
and deployment gates), is a separate comparison with its own qualification query, and does not
enter the headline.\cl{PR16}
\end{description}

\subsection{Amendment of 2026-10-16}\label{sec:amend}

Before any run, the registration was amended in three respects; the original text is
unchanged and the amendment is dated below it.\cl{PR14} \emph{First}, the student of CE4 is
trained on verified trajectories produced by the 27B teacher on the development partition, not
on its own. The reason is a pilot observation, made on one repository family under a harness
that this amendment replaces and cited only as the reason: the untrained 8B model almost never
edited a file, so its own verified trajectories are too few to train on (lane notes
\code{runs/l1-measure/results.md} and \code{runs/l1-measure/rerun/results.md} on the project
drive). The training-data source and the arms of the test change; the partitions, the
primary test, the size rule, retention, the exclusions, the stop rules and the success
condition do not.\cl{PR15} \emph{Second}, CE8 is registered as the 27B counterpart, with the same
protocol.\cl{PR16} \emph{Third}, one harness profile is pinned for all experiments (episode
bounds and a follow-up for an unchanged tree, \cref{sec:protocol}), and results measured
before it are excluded from all analyses.\cl{PR14}
A second amendment, also before any run, registers the run parameters: evaluation arms are greedy with one attempt per task, the exam seed and the 46 qualification and 34 development families, the serving flags, the LoRA recipe and the commit equivalence for the pinned fixes; it does not change the primary test or the success condition.\cl{PR17}
A third amendment, before any qualification query or training run, follows from the first teacher run: the solver's shell had no filesystem confinement, and in 27 of 61 episodes the 27B searched the host, read the original task sources outside its working tree and listed other attempts, which the exposure audit as registered did not flag. That run is withdrawn; every workspace command is now confined to its tree by the kernel, a tool call naming a path outside the tree marks the episode exposed, the training set leaves out and counts what would cross the exam seal, a solve is bounded at one hour of wall time, and the CE7 repository is named (sqlglot); the primary test and the success condition are unchanged.\cl{PR18}

\paragraph{What a failure would show.} An adapter that does not win (for CE4, one distilled from the teacher's verified trajectories), a gain that a different
repository's adapter or unverified trajectories match, a retention loss beyond the margin, or
a served adapter that disagrees with the in-process one are each stated outcomes of the
registration, and each is publishable as such. A development gain with no qualification gain
(sign test $p>0.2$) is reported as memorisation. If fewer than 25 non-tied families are
available the comparison is labelled a pilot and the rule is not loosened.\cl{PR08}
