\section{The headline experiment and its replication: registered, and what has run}\label{sec:pending}

\paragraph{The claim this paper exists to establish.} Verified engineering-agent experience
can produce a sustained improvement in completion of previously unseen repository tasks,
under a controlled compute budget and without unacceptable loss of earlier capability.\cl{PR09}
\textbf{That claim is not yet measured.} The teacher's first pass (CE1) and an
underpowered pilot of the headline (CE4) have run and are reported at the end of this section;
CE8 was trained and stopped by its memorisation guard before any evaluation. This section states
what is registered, what each outcome will mean and where its numbers will appear; apart from
the paragraphs marked as results, nothing in it is a result.

The experiments are registered in \code{prereg/coding-tasks.md}, dated 2026-10-16 and written
before any of their runs; this paper states the registration as it stands, and
\cref{app:deviations} lists every dated change to it with the outcome each preceded.
\Cref{tab:registered} lists them. Their
numbers will be read from \code{data/results.csv} by \code{scripts/build_data.py}; a row exists
only for a finished run, and the status column below changes by itself when one does.\cl{PL02}

\begin{description}
\item[CE1, the untrained agent.] The base agent's resolve rate on repository tasks, its failure
profile, its cost per resolved task and the discordance between the base and the 27B teacher per
family, which is the sizing input for the qualification partition (greedy decoding makes two seeds of one arm identical). No threshold; nothing is trained before it is
reported.\cl{PR10}
\item[CE4, the headline.] A LoRA adapter on Qwen3-8B trained only on verified
trajectories, produced by the 27B teacher served as the base, from
development-partition families, compared with the untrained 8B on the qualification partition by the
primary test of \cref{sec:protocol}, served through Brain and driven by Sven, with retention,
serve-verdict agreement and the three controls. The headline holds only if the sign test has
$p<0.05$, retention is not refused and agreement is within the serve check's tolerance; any
other outcome is reported as what it is, including ``no held-out improvement'' and
``memorisation''.\cl{PR11,PR15}
\item[CE6, the 27B model.] Ten stages from loading the exact model to comparing correctness,
tool-call validity and resources against the base, gated by training correctness and by
deployment agreement. The 27B is not used for a confirmatory query until both gates pass;
the training rate of \cref{sec:throughput} is reported as measured and no target is
registered.\cl{PR12}
\item[CE7, replication.] The unchanged CE1 and CE4 procedure on a second, independent
repository chosen by a rule fixed in advance (permissive licence, offline build, a suite of
bounded running time, no part of the pipeline tuned on it), with the cross cells that separate
learning a repository from learning the protocol. The headline counts as reproduced only if
both repositories pass the primary test independently.\cl{PR13}
\item[CE8, 27B self-improvement.] A LoRA adapter on the bf16 base of the 27B model trained on its
own verified, exposure-clean trajectories of the development sets (rank 16, alpha 32, learning
rate $5\times10^{-5}$, $B=2$ records per step, $\lceil 2R/B\rceil$ steps for the $R$ records the trainer
reports, a held-out monitoring share of 0.2 by whole family read eight times over the budget,
stopping after two evaluations without improvement), held to a memorisation guard
(a run whose final training loss is below half its first-step value while its monitoring loss is
above its minimum is not evaluated), served through the adapter bank of the 8-bit teacher server,
and compared with the 27B base on the qualification halves under the solve protocol of
\cref{sec:protocol}. A second candidate, CE8-T, is trained the same way on a teacher pass recorded
with a 1024-token thinking budget. A shuffled-instruction control, CE8-S, keeps format, tools and
token count and breaks the pairing of trajectory and task; learning is claimed only if CE8 beats
both the base and CE8-S on the primary test. CE8 is a separate comparison and does not enter the
headline.\cl{PR16,PR34,PR36,PR37,PR38,PR39}
\end{description}

\paragraph{Changes that bear on the evidence.} Four of the dated changes of
\cref{app:deviations} change what the results can mean. \emph{First}, the headline's student
trains on verified trajectories of the 27B teacher rather than its own, because in a pilot under
an earlier harness the untrained 8B model almost never edited a file (lane notes
\code{runs/l1-measure/results.md} on the project drive); the partitions, the primary test and
the success condition did not change.\cl{PR14,PR15} \emph{Second}, the first teacher run was
withdrawn: its shell had no filesystem confinement, and in 27 of 61 episodes the 27B searched the
host and read task sources outside its tree, which the exposure audit as then registered did not
flag; every workspace command has since been confined by the kernel and a call naming a path
outside the tree marks the episode exposed.\cl{PR18} \emph{Third}, a defect in brain's backward
pass scaled every 27B training gradient by about $4\times10^{-6}$ until it was fixed, so every 27B
training before the fix, including the learning-rate sweep the CE8 recipe first relied on, is no
basis for any value.\cl{PR36,PR38} \emph{Fourth}, the CE8 training run tripped the registered
memorisation guard, so its adapter is not evaluated; one further run on a one-pass budget was
allowed with the guard unchanged and no later retry.\cl{PR40}

\paragraph{CE4, the first confirmatory pilot: not shown.} The registered query could be run only on a subset (\cref{app:deviations}), under the protocol in force before the solve protocol of \cref{sec:protocol} (greedy decoding, one attempt per task): one task of each of the 23 families of the first qualification half, solved once by each arm with both served by Brain on the same build and tier. The adapter was trained on the verified, exposure-clean trajectories of the teacher's first pass, projected one tool call per turn. Of the 23 tasks, 21 are paired; the untrained 8B resolves one and the adapter none, so the sign test has a single discordant family against the adapter and does not support the claim; the comparison is a pilot by the registered rule and is reported as \emph{not shown}.\cl{M47,PR24} It is a weak test: with the untrained model near zero there is almost no room for a gain, and the adapter's attempts mostly end at the harness's repetition stop. The step count (47) is not the registered rule's (29 for the 114 training records the trainer reports), and the controls, the second qualification half, retention and the serve-agreement sample were not run.

\paragraph{CE8: trained, stopped by its guard, not evaluated.} The registered CE8 training ran
and stopped early at step 51: its training loss fell from 0.1476 at the first
step to 0.0221 at the last, and its best monitoring loss, 0.0839 at step 27 against 0.1101 for the
base, rose to 0.0867 by the last evaluation, so the registered guard calls it memorised.\cl{M48}
The one further run the registration allowed, on a one-pass budget, behaves the same way at a
smaller scale: best monitoring loss 0.0829 at step 30, 0.0832 at its last evaluation, training loss
0.1476 to 0.0282, memorised by the same rule.\cl{M49} Neither adapter is evaluated, and no claim
about CE8 follows from either. The guard reads a single step's training loss on two records and any
rise at the last evaluation, so it fires on noise of this size; the registration keeps it as
written, and the lower monitoring loss than the base's is a training-side quantity, not evidence of
a gain on any task.

\paragraph{What a failure would show.} An adapter that does not win (for CE4, one distilled from the teacher's verified trajectories), a gain that a different
repository's adapter or unverified trajectories match, a retention loss beyond the margin, or
a served adapter that disagrees with the in-process one are each stated outcomes of the
registration, and each is publishable as such. A development gain with no qualification gain
(sign test $p>0.2$) is reported as memorisation. If fewer than 25 non-tied families are
available the comparison is labelled a pilot and the rule is not loosened.\cl{PR08}
