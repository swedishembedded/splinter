\begin{abstract}
A local model can keep learning from a team's documents, tools and repositories only if the path
from a source to a released model can be inspected stage by stage and its effect is measured on
material the learner never saw. We describe a self-learning loop built from three cooperating
projects, Splinter (what is learned), Brain (training and serving) and Sven (agents): code admits
the tasks a model proposes, an agent attempts them, typed verifiers keep what they confirm, verified
experience becomes training data, and a candidate is released only through a four-check gate, with
immutable releases and rollback. We report what the loop did under tests registered before their
runs, including those that did not show a gain. On repository tasks the registered confirmatory query
did not show an 8B adapter distilled from a 27B teacher better than its base (18 families won, 12 lost,
$p=0.18$), while a same-seed contrast of the supervision did separate (26 won, 11 lost, $p=0.010$).\cl{QUAL1}
Two promoted generations of a 27B model trained on its own agent experience did not beat their base
on one measurement.\cl{G2703,G2704} With no teacher, a 4B model learned a language's standard library
from the raw manual, and whether it, its base or a 27B model wrote its tasks did not change the
gain.\cl{SL23} Four registered series then asked whether a model teaching itself improves at every
generation on an exam it never reads, judged by an independent examiner. Three did not meet their
criterion. The fourth did, in all four lineages: Qwen3-4B, retrained from the base each generation on
the interpreter-verified frontier of its own Lua~5.1 programs, beat its predecessor at each of three
generations on 889 items its base could not answer, rising from 12 items right to between 192 and
241, with the weakest step won 67 to 36 ($p=0.00146$).\cl{RS28,RS29} Every statement about the system
or a measurement carries an id in a claims ledger that is re-checked against the three repositories at
pinned commits or against the raw records.
\end{abstract}
