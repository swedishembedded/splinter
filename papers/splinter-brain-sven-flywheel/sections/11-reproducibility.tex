\section{Reproducibility}\label{sec:repro}

\paragraph{Verification.} \code{make -C papers/splinter-brain-sven-flywheel verify} first runs the
specifications of the pin rule and of the record parsers, then re-runs every structure check against
the commits in \code{data/commits.json}, then \code{scripts/build_data.py --check}, which resolves
each measured and computed claim to its value, requires the claim text to contain the value, requires
every ledger row to be cited in the text and every citation to be in the ledger, and checks that the
pooled steps computed from the examiner's lineage verdicts equal its plan verdicts. The ledger holds
\mLedgerClaims{} claims, \mLedgerStructure{} of them structure claims backed by \mLedgerChecks{}
checks. A pin must be contained in a remote-tracking ref of its repository, so a fresh clone of the
three remotes reproduces the result; the sibling repositories are given by \code{--sven} and
\code{--brain}. \code{make check} includes this verification as \code{check/papers}. The bibliography
entries were checked against the archive's abstract pages on 2026-11-15.

\paragraph{Draft and final.} The paper's status is recorded in \code{status.txt}; when it reads
final, \code{build\_data.py --check} also fails while any registered-but-unmeasured or planned claim
remains, so a final version holds only structure facts, measurements, computations from measurements
and citations. This version is a draft because one planned claim remains: the two registered RSI4
follow-ups, whose results it will report when they exist.\cl{RS39}

\paragraph{Raw records.} The raw logs are not kept in the repository. \code{data/measurements.json}
records, per log, the values the claims cite, the exact command, the Brain commit and the log's
SHA-256, and \code{make verify EVIDENCE=DIR} re-extracts the values from the logs found in \code{DIR}
and compares digests and values. The records of the documentation runs, the 27B generations and every
RSI series are published in the evidence dataset \code{splinter-flywheel-evidence} under the paths
\code{data/measurements.json} names: for RSI2 to RSI4 the examiner's signed step and plan verdicts and
the registered rule it applied, the learner's round records, each candidate's record and list of
trained programs and, for RSI4, the twelve champion adapters; for RSI1 each arm's registered analysis
and per-model grades. The qualification logs of the training substrate and the logs of the coding-task
pilots and probes were kept with the runs that made them; their values, digests and commands are in
\code{data/measurements.json}.

\paragraph{The examiner.} The examiner of RSI2 to RSI4 is specified in \cref{sec:rsi-design} at the
level needed to build an equivalent one: exam custody, the one-way hand-off and its two files, the
training-overlap exclusion, the direct-completion protocol (request, decoding, answer extraction and
comparison, the token limit and the treatment of a cut reply), serving every model on one pinned build
and tier with the base identified from the checkpoint's own files, the per-step exact one-sided sign
test, the per-lineage plan rule and aggregate-only signed verdicts. With the published candidate
records, trained-program lists and adapters, and the exam file released with RSI1, an equivalent
examiner re-grades every RSI4 champion.

\paragraph{Registrations.} All registrations are in the paper's \code{prereg/} directory: the
coding-task protocol in \code{coding-tasks.md}, the acquisition experiments in \code{acquisition.md},
RSI1 in \code{recursive-improvement.md}, and RSI2 to RSI4 each in its own file (\code{rsi4.md} for
RSI4) with a launch record (\code{rsi4-launch.md}) that fixes builds, binary digests, settings and
seeds before the first generation and carries the series' dated deviations; the outcome of each series
is appended to its registration.

\paragraph{Build.} From the repository root the paper is built by
\begin{center}\code{make papers/splinter-brain-sven-flywheel/pdf}\end{center}
into \code{target/papers}; every table, plot and number of the RSI series and of the coding-task pilots
is generated from \code{data/} into the build directory.
