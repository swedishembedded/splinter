\section{Limitations}\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{What the result is about.} One task family (predicting what Lua~5.1 programs print), one base
model, three rounds of LoRA training on interpreter labels, and no tools and no reasoning: thinking is
off everywhere and the training records are answers only. S4 improves direct prediction without
thinking; nothing is claimed about other tasks, models, longer series or reasoning, where the base with
thinking on may already do far better.

\paragraph{What the statistics support.} Four lineages are encouraging, not a reliability estimate.
``Every generation improves'' is the registrations' strict criterion, not the measure of useful learning;
the learning curves of \cref{fig:scaling} are. On the fresh exam the first round gains nothing and the
registered 3-of-4 criteria are not met; the third round holds in three lineages by the clustered bound.
The advantage of the rounds over matched base-proposed data is inconclusive, and M is matched in data,
not in compute.\cl{FU03,FU07,FU09} The exam that guided the design was reused; the fresh exam was drawn
afterwards, but its templates include those of the old exam.\cl{CT22} The overlap exclusion catches
exact and normalised matches only, and the similarity audit shows the fresh exam lies close to the
training programs (median token-set Jaccard 0.54).\cl{FU06} The examiner is a separate program run by the
same operators, and its symmetric signature protects the verdicts against the learner, not against the
authors. The learner's dev gate advanced on any net gain and filtered nothing.\cl{RS49}

\paragraph{Not continual learning.} Each round retrains from the base, a consolidation baseline rather
than a solution for an unbounded stream: an adapter loses 14 to 30\% of the items its predecessor
answered, and a retention test of what each round acquired is not implemented. The seed-null retrains
show that this churn is mostly training noise at the level of items while lineage means are
stable.\cl{RS48,FU07,FU08}

\paragraph{The rest of the paper.} The limits of the system specification and of the coding, 27B and
documentation experiments are in \cref{app:limits}.

\paragraph{Open directions.} The questions the evidence leaves are richer oracle supervision (execution
traces, compact verified reasoning), a proposer-by-selector design to separate who proposes from who
selects, compute-matched baselines, a learnability rather than a failure curriculum, and a retention
matrix over acquired capabilities.
