\section{Deviations from the registration}\label{app:deviations}

The protocol of \cref{sec:protocol} is the registration in \code{prereg/coding-tasks.md}, fixed before
any coding-task run. \Cref{tab:deviations,tab:deviations2,tab:deviations3} list the dated changes made in this paper after the
registration; ``Before'' names the earliest outcome a change preceded. The self-improvement series
(\cref{sec:rsi}) record their own deviations, dated, in their launch records and outcome entries, and
\cref{tab:deviations-rsi} lists them.
