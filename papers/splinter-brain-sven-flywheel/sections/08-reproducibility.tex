\section{Reproducibility}\label{sec:repro}

\paragraph{Verification.} \code{make -C papers/splinter-brain-sven-flywheel verify} re-runs
every structure check against the commits in \code{data/commits.json} (Splinter
\code{2aed856d}, Sven \code{20927f79}, Brain \code{9b495d21}) and then
\code{scripts/build_data.py --check}, which resolves each measured claim to its raw record,
requires the claim text to contain the value, and requires every ledger row to be cited in the
text and every citation to be in the ledger. The ledger is \code{data/claims.csv}; the
bibliography entries were checked against the archive's abstract pages on 2026-10-16.

\paragraph{Experiments.} E1 and E2/E3 are run by \code{scripts/run_experiments.sh} (the
exact commands), with the Splinter binary built from the main checkout after the repair of
its build against the current Sven and Brain (Splinter \code{ce42a55} plus uncommitted
repairs, Sven \code{02adbda7}, Brain \code{dc50b7cc}; binary sha256 prefix \code{f91620c5}).
The serve check, had it been reached, would have used a Brain release binary built on
2026-10-07, whose commit we did not record. Models: Qwen3-0.6B (policy) and Qwen3-4B
(generator, teacher), served by Brain on a shared NVIDIA GH200. The seeds drawn by the runs
are in the raw records (denoise seed in \code{data/runs/e1/tasks.json}, fact seed in
\code{data/runs/e2/build.json}, training seeds in \code{data/runs/e2/raw/commands.log}). The
protocol was fixed before the runs (\code{prereg/protocol.md}). Deviations from it: E1 ran
with the 45-minute limit unused; E2 and E3 produced no release, so the registered
success criteria were not met and none of its statistical tests could be evaluated.

\paragraph{Training qualification (E4).} The logs in \code{data/runs/qual} are Brain's
qualification logs at commit \mEFourCommit{}; each log's header records its command.
E4 contains only the runs that finished; runs that were planned and not run are not
reported as results.

\paragraph{Build.} \code{make papers/splinter-brain-sven-flywheel/pdf} builds the paper to
\code{target/papers}; the tables and numbers are generated from \code{data/runs} into the
build directory.
