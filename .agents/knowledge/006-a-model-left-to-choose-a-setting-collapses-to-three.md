# 006. A model left to choose a setting collapses to three

Asked to design a present-day situation "in a field far from his", the
helper put 116 of 130 scenarios in software teams, a hospital or a school
board, one scenario per principle. A student trained on that learns "this
field, this layout", and a benchmark drawn from the same fields cannot show
it.

Assign the field in code from a fixed list by hash, check the draft uses a
word of that field, make a principle's second scenario the opposite kind to
its first so it is seen both holding and not holding, and hold a few fields
out of training entirely: they form an out-of-domain slice that goes to a
separate benchmark file, never into the frozen one.
